As CEO, Focus On These 5 Things
During the meeting, write down:
Problem
What hurts?
Money
How does the business make money?
Process
How does work flow?
People
Who uses the system?
Priority
What must be built first?

One Critical Mistake to Avoid
Do NOT promise:

Exact timeline

Exact cost

Exact features

during the meeting.
Say:

"We'll analyze the requirements internally and provide a detailed proposal with scope, timeline, and cost."

That keeps you professional and protects you from underestimating the project.
For this first meeting, your target should be:
80% listening, 20% talking.
