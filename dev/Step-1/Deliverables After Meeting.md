# Deliverables after discovery

**Owner:** Product manager. **Purpose:** Convert meeting evidence into a reviewable plan before making a binding offer.

## Handoff pack

| Deliverable | Contents | Destination | Reviewer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery summary | Business model, current workflow, validated pain points, evidence gaps | Phases 1-2 and Stage 1 | Sponsor + operations |
| Business requirements | Actors, journeys, functional IDs, business rules, quality targets, acceptance | Stage 2 | Product owner |
| Prioritized feature list | Release 1, Release 2, exclusions, assumptions | Stage 3 | Product owner + sponsor |
| Solution design | Chosen stack, module boundaries, data authority, failure handling, security and recovery | Stage 4 | Technical lead |
| Delivery estimate | Work packages, role capacity, dependencies, range and contingency | Stage 5 | Delivery manager |
| Indicative proposal | Deliverables, price method, milestones, operating costs, support assumptions | Stage 6 | Sponsor |
| Decision and risk record | Unknowns, owners, due gates, mitigations | README and Stage 1 | All accountable owners |

## Suggested post-meeting sequence

Within two business days, consolidate notes and request missing evidence. Then run the internal review, draft requirements and scope, resolve high-impact assumptions, and prepare architecture and estimation. A simple review might take several days; complex business rules or unavailable evidence require more time. Original day labels are not a guarantee that production planning can be completed in three days.

Do not distribute unverified examples as approved requirements. Do not send a final quotation until its scope, assumptions, acceptance conditions, and dependencies are visible.

## Handoff checklist

- [ ] Company, business model, operational owners, and decision makers recorded.
- [ ] Pain points distinguish observed evidence from hypotheses.
- [ ] Required features have IDs and observable acceptance conditions.
- [ ] Payments, inventory, refunds, delivery, and policy gaps have owners.
- [ ] Stack alternatives have a proposed launch choice.
- [ ] Estimate includes QA, operations, migration, training, and contingency.
- [ ] Recurring costs and third-party approvals are separate from delivery effort.
- [ ] Unresolved decisions are marked open; no customer meeting or signoff is implied.
