# Stage 7: Solution and proposal review meeting

**Owner:** Product/delivery manager. **Participants:** Sponsor, product owner, operations, finance, technical lead. **Timebox:** 75-90 minutes.

## Meeting objective

Validate the proposed business workflow, scope, operating responsibilities, delivery range, and commercial structure. Seek specific decisions with evidence. No meeting or approval is implied by this document.

## Pre-read

Share the planning index, requirements, scope, architecture summary, estimate, proposal, and open decision register. Ask attendees to review their policy and dependency responsibilities, especially merchant onboarding, refund authority, delivery rules, catalog data, budget, and launch timing.

## Agenda

| Minutes | Topic | Decision sought |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-10 | Business model and pain points | Correct assumptions and rank outcomes |
| 10-30 | Customer and staff journey walkthrough | Validate purchase, fulfillment, return and finance behavior |
| 30-45 | Release 1 boundaries and exclusions | Confirm essentials and later candidates |
| 45-60 | Delivery roadmap and dependencies | Confirm staff/reviewer availability and deadline flexibility |
| 60-75 | Cost method, recurring costs and support | Agree next commercial inputs and budget constraints |
| 75-90 | Risks, open decisions and next steps | Assign owners and decision dates |

## Scenarios to walk through

A normal purchase; the last unit bought by two customers; successful payment without browser return; payment after reservation expiry; shipment failure; partial return/refund; finance settlement mismatch.

For each, explain what the customer sees, what staff must do, which control prevents a mistake, and which policy remains undecided. Use architecture details only where they explain cost, risk, or an operating choice.

## Review questions to settle

- Are Bangladesh/BDT, one merchant/warehouse, English UI and guest checkout correct?
- Is manual courier tracking acceptable, and is COD required at launch?
- Who approves tax/invoice, return eligibility and refunds?
- Are the data allowance and exclusions realistic?
- Can the proposed role capacity and external dependencies be supplied?
- Does the budget cover both delivery and ongoing operations?

## Meeting record and exit

Record decisions, objections, changes, unanswered items, accountable owners and due dates. Mark scope as accepted for contracting, revise and return, or pause pending business inputs. Silence is not approval.

After the meeting, update affected documents together and issue a concise decision summary through the agreed human communication process. This planning task does not send messages to stakeholders.
