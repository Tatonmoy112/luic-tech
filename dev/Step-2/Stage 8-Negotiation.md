# Stage 8: Negotiation framework

**Owner:** Sponsor/commercial lead with delivery manager. **Goal:** Agree a sustainable delivery arrangement with measurable boundaries.

## Internal preparation

Determine a target price, minimum sustainable price, and conditions under which to decline or redesign scope. These figures cannot be filled truthfully until role rates, delivery capacity, overhead, business risk and desired margin are known.

| Item | Preparation required |
| --- | --- |
| Cost floor | Role-based base effort, nonrecoverable overhead, delivery obligations and risk allowance |
| Target fee | Sustainable margin and agreed business value, without invented savings claims |
| Payment risk | Deposit, milestone timing, invoice period, dispute handling, exposure limit |
| Scope flexibility | Named optional outcomes with quantified removal/addition impact |
| Schedule flexibility | Deadline options, pilot approach, staffing and dependency impact |
| Support obligations | Funded hours, severity definitions, escalation, defect boundary |
| Exit conditions | Unfunded scope, impossible deadlines, unclear acceptance, unavailable required owners |

## Negotiation tradeoffs

| Request | Professional response |
| --- | --- |
| Lower total fee | Reduce named optional scope, rollout breadth, or custom presentation effort and re-estimate |
| Earlier launch | Consider a smaller pilot/release and confirm role bottlenecks; preserve integrity gates |
| More features at same price | Propose an explicit scope swap with acceptance and effort impact |
| Fixed price before discovery | Offer a bounded discovery milestone, then a clearer fixed baseline |
| Reduced advance | Review cash-flow exposure and smaller delivery milestones |
| Unlimited changes or support | Define change allowance, service hours, exclusions, and approval process |
| Move to cheaper hosting | Compare service targets, operations labor and recovery tradeoffs; record architecture decision |

## Protected launch outcomes

Do not bargain away server-side payment verification, duplicate-safe financial actions, transactional stock controls, record authorization, essential audit, backup restoration, or acceptance verification. If the project cannot fund these, redesign the release and commercial model before proceeding.

Do not discount by assuming unpaid overtime or by hiding third-party charges. Contingency covers identified uncertainty; removing it changes the risk allocation and must be explicit.

## Negotiation record

For every concession, record the original term, proposed change, business reason, reciprocal commitment, cost/schedule/support impact, decision owner and affected document version. Resolve changes into one final scope and proposal; do not leave conflicting terms across email notes or draft documents.

Complete when scope, fee method/amount, milestones, responsibilities, change handling, support and acceptance are aligned. Legal drafting and signatures follow in Stage 9.
