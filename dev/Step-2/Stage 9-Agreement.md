# Stage 9: Agreement and authorization checklist

**Owner:** Sponsor and commercial/contract reviewer. **Status:** Planning checklist, not a contract or legal advice.

## Purpose

Ensure the eventual written agreement reflects the approved scope, estimate, account ownership, acceptance, operating responsibilities, and commercial terms. No contract has been prepared or signed by this documentation task.

## Agreement schedule

| Topic | Required decision or attachment |
| --- | --- |
| Parties | Correct legal entities, addresses, authorized signatories and notices |
| Scope | Versioned Stage 2/3 baseline, exclusions, data allowance, supported environments |
| Deliverables | Product, technical and handover outcomes with explicit acceptance criteria |
| Schedule | Relative roadmap, kickoff readiness, dependencies, reviewer response periods |
| Price | Agreed fee/rate model, contingency treatment, vendor costs, commercial taxes and invoicing |
| Milestones | Evidence and named reviewer for each Stage 6 payment trigger |
| Acceptance | Review period, written acceptance/rejection with criterion, correction and resubmission |
| Change control | Request, impact assessment, approver, updated scope/cost/date |
| Customer inputs | Brand/content, data quality, policies, provider onboarding, staff access |
| Intellectual property | Ownership/license treatment of custom deliverables, reusable assets and third-party dependencies |
| Accounts and access | Merchant ownership of AWS, Auth0, gateway, domain and repository; delegated access and handover |
| Data handling | Approved access, processors, locations, retention, deletion and incident obligations |
| Support | Hypercare, defect correction, service hours, escalation and separate maintenance scope |
| Confidentiality/publicity | Use of business information and separate permission for case studies |
| Delay/dispute/termination | Review and remedies, payment for accepted work, handover and access removal |
| Liability and governing terms | Business-approved terms reviewed by the appropriate contract adviser |

This checklist does not assert jurisdiction-specific legal requirements, statutory tax treatment, liability limits or required retention periods. Those terms need the parties' own qualified review.

## Proposed acceptance workflow

Delivery team submits milestone evidence mapped to agreed requirement IDs. Named reviewer accepts or provides criterion-specific rejection within the agreed period. The team resolves defects and resubmits. Disputed new requirements go through change control.

No response is not automatically deemed acceptance in this plan. Payment dates, acceptance consequences, and delay handling must be explicitly agreed.

## Ready-kickoff record

- [ ] Contracting entities and authorized signatories confirmed.
- [ ] Scope, assumptions, exclusions, quality targets and estimate versions attached.
- [ ] Fee, payment triggers and third-party billing responsibilities agreed.
- [ ] Roles, team allocations, availability and decision delegates named.
- [ ] Account ownership, onboarding and data/content responsibilities accepted.
- [ ] Support and handover funding/coverage agreed.
- [ ] Required signature and initial commercial condition satisfied.
- [ ] Remaining dependencies recorded with owners and escalation dates.

All items remain pending. A completed planning document is not implementation authorization or a production launch approval.
